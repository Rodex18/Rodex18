## 🛡️ Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity

Game integrity and combating exploiters are among my top technical priorities. I am currently building a proprietary ecosystem of smart tools:

* **Jarvis:** An AI-powered personal assistant designed to streamline workflows.
* **Thaurus (In Development):** An advanced **AI-based global Anti-Cheat system** built upon Jarvis's architecture. Its goal is to detect real-time anomalies (fly, noclip, speed hacking, physics manipulation) via constant server-side monitoring without causing latency.
* **Discord Integration:** Custom bots developed in **JavaScript** connected via webhooks/API to Roblox servers to send detailed alerts (embeds featuring player ID, specific infraction, accumulated history, local timestamp, and avatar) with a focus on fair moderation and human review.
